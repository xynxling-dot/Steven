About

A catering house built around care

Regale began with a simple belief: an excellent event starts with thoughtful food and careful preparation.

The short version

EXPERIENCE THE DIFFERENCE: Today Regale creates menus around the season, the venue, and the people gathered around the table, with one team accountable for how the day actually runs.

Regale started in 2016 as a small catering kitchen serving family gatherings around Angeles City. Since then, the team has grown through word of mouth, one carefully planned event at a time.
